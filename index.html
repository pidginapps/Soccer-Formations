Dotted ring: where they stand when the ball is in the center circle. The arrow shows how far the ball's position moved them.

Creative passes add yellow curves: a curl round a defender, a ball over the top (broken curve), or a ball into space for a runner (the ring is where it lands, the dots are the run; tap the ring to play it there). Pass lines: a solid line means the pass is on. A thin line means it is on but long (over 22 yards). A dotted line means a defender is close enough to cut it out. Field drawn 60 × 40 yd. Build-out lines sit halfway between the penalty box and midfield, and in 7v7 nobody can be offside between halfway and the build-out line.
